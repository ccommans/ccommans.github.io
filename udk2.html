Upside Down Kingdom 2

This is a collection of the weekly handouts for our UDK2 study through the Sermon on the Mount. Each handout is my personal notes from the week's study and is roughly how I guide our group discussion. There is no set format, but generally I like to annotate the passage's structure, compare different translations side by side, note any relevant Old Testament passages, and list the questions I had wrestled with. My goal for our group is to be better readers of Scripture through analysis and discussion of the text before turning to application. If there is anything specific you would like me to add here, please let me know; this is supposed to be a resource for you!
